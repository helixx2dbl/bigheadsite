01 — The brand

What we make

BigHead Builder turns any photo into a giant printed head on a stick. The customer uploads a picture, we cut the head out, print it at about 24″ tall on rigid board, hand-assemble it and ship it flat to their door.

Who buys them

Sports crowds, race finish lines, tailgates, birthdays, bachelor and bachelorette parties, graduations, send-offs. Groups more often than individuals.

The feeling

Loud, warm and funny. This is a product people buy to make a friend laugh from fifty feet away. Never corporate, never ironic, never mean.

The promise

You see the exact cut-out before you pay. A person checks every head before it prints.

The one-line positioning

Turn any photo into a giant head on a stick. Upload a photo, we cut out the head, hand-assemble it, and ship it to your door.

02 — Logo

Three marks, one family

All three are the same illustration. Pick by context, never redraw.

Sticker mark
Primary. The white keyline lets it sit on photography.
Badge
No keyline. Use on flat colour only.
Head only
Decorative. Never the sole identifier.

Do

  • Keep clear space of at least half the badge’s diameter on all sides.
  • Minimum size 0.5″ / 48px wide. Below that the curved lettering fills in.
  • Use the supplied SVGs. They scale to billboard without redrawing.
  • On busy photography, use the sticker mark so the keyline separates it.

Don’t

  • Recolour it. The mark is berry, teal and cream — no mono or single-colour versions exist.
  • Stretch, rotate, skew, add shadows, outlines or gradients.
  • Put the badge on a busy photo without the sticker keyline.
  • Set the wordmark in type. “Big Head Builder” inside the badge is drawn artwork.
  • Use the head alone where the brand must be identified.

Space and size

Minimum size

comfortable
0.5″ — floor

Below half an inch (48px) the curved lettering and the sunglasses fill in. If it has to go smaller than this, use the head mark and put the name in type beside it.

On photography

Over a photo, always use the sticker mark — its white keyline is what separates it from whatever is behind it. If the picture is busy enough that the keyline still is not enough, put the mark on a solid cream or ink panel instead of fighting the image.

Proportion

Roughly 70% cream, 20% ink, 10% berry and teal combined. The accents work because they are rare. A berry-heavy layout reads as a sale flyer.

Accessibility. Every pairing in this system clears WCAG AA for body text: Ink on Cream 12.12:1, Berry Deep on Cream 7.84:1, Teal Deep on Cream 7.01:1, Berry on Cream 5.62:1, Cream on Ink 12.12:1. Values are measured, not estimated. For CMYK, convert from these RGB values with your printer’s profile rather than guessing a build.

Rules

  • Body copy is 600 weight, not 400. The brand reads soft and heavy.
  • Headlines are 900 with slightly negative tracking.
  • Sentence case for headlines. Uppercase only for small eyebrow labels.

Never

  • Set body copy in Pacifico. It is a logo face; it is unreadable in paragraphs.
  • Substitute another rounded sans. Nunito is free — there is no reason to.
  • Use Nunito 400 for anything but the lightest captions.

05 — Voice

Short, warm, a bit daft

Write like a friend who is genuinely excited about the stupid thing they made you. Confident, plain and short. The product is funny, so the copy does not have to work at it.

Real lines from the site

“Your face. Getting big…”
“Giant heads on a stick, built from your photos.”
“Preview it BIG”
“Ready to get big?”
“Paying for the whole crew?”
“They scan, they save. You get the same back.”

Sentence shape

Most lines are under eight words. Verbs up front. Contractions always. One idea per sentence, then stop — the next line does the next idea.

Sounds like us

  • “Upload a photo. We’ll do the rest.”
  • “Impossible to miss from the bleachers.”
  • “See exactly what you’re getting before you pay.”
  • Specific and concrete: 24 inches, ten seconds, eight to a box.

Doesn’t

  • “Elevate your game-day experience.” — marketing voice.
  • “Revolutionary AI-powered cutout technology.” — we don’t sell the machinery.
  • Punching down, roasting, or anything at someone’s expense.
  • Exclamation marks in every line. One is a joke; four is shouting.

Naming

BigHead Builder — one word “BigHead”, capital B and H, space before Builder. A single product is a BigHead. Not “Big Head Builder”, not “Bighead”, not “BHB”. The drawn wordmark inside the logo sets it as three words — that is artwork, and it is the one exception.

06 — Photography

Real crowds, real phones

Every photo we use is a real customer holding a real head. Nothing is staged in a studio and nothing is stock. That is the whole look — if it could be an advert for anything else, it is wrong.

Look for

  • Daylight, outdoors, a crowd or a group.
  • The head held up on its stick, at or above head height.
  • Faces laughing, arms up, motion. Phone-camera texture is fine and wanted.
  • Events: races, stands, tailgates, parties.

Avoid

  • Studio lighting, seamless backdrops, product-on-white.
  • Stock photography of any kind.
  • Heavy filters or colour grades that fight the cream palette.
  • A head lying flat on a table — it reads as a print, not a product.

Motion, if you make video

The brand has two signature movements, both already built: a squash-and-stretch bounce (1150ms, widest and shortest at the ground, perfectly round at the apex) and a gentle sway on the stick (2600ms, about three degrees, pivoting at the bottom of the stick as if held). Keep it small — a few degrees is a wave, ten is a seizure.

07 — Claims

What ads may and may not say

These are the facts checkout will actually honour, as of September 2026. Everything here is enforced in code — if an ad promises otherwise, the customer finds out at the payment step.

FactSay it like this
Price$25 for the first print of a design; $17.50 for each extra copy of that same design (30% off).
The discountPer design, not per order. Three different faces is $25 each — it is not a volume tier.
ShippingFlat $5 per box. One box holds up to 8 heads, so eight ship for the same $5 as one.
SizeAbout 24 inches tall, roughly three times life size, on rigid lightweight board.
AssemblyShips flat; the customer tapes the stick on with supplied 3M tape. About ten seconds.
TurnaroundBuilt in 1–2 business days, then 2–6 days in transit. US only.
ReferralEvery order ships with a QR code. A friend scans it and saves 10%; the referrer is refunded that same amount.

Do not claim

  • Free shipping. It was free until 10 September 2026. It is now $5 per box.
  • Rush, express or guaranteed delivery. There is no expedited option at checkout and no date field. Customers with a hard date are told to get in touch first.
  • Bulk or volume discounts — “5 for the crew”, “20 for the section”. The only discount is the per-design duplicate price.
  • “Get 10% of your order back” for referrals. The referrer gets 10% of their friend’s order, not their own.
  • International shipping. United States only at present.
  • Accounts, logins or order history. There are none — orders are found by email.

If a price or policy changes, this page is the one to re-check. The live figures are held in a single file in the site codebase (lib/pricing.ts) and every page reads from it, so the site can be trusted as the current source of truth.
